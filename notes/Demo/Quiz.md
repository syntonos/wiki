# Quiz

Single answer:

```quiz
Q: What is the capital of France?
- [ ] Lyon
- [x] Paris
- [ ] Nice
? Paris has been the capital for centuries.
```

Multiple answers:

```quiz
Q: Which of these are prime numbers?
- [x] 2
- [ ] 9
- [x] 11
- [ ] 15
? 9 = 3×3 and 15 = 3×5.
```

Math works in questions, options and explanations, and `Shuffle: yes` randomizes the option order:

```quiz
Q: What is $\int_0^1 2x\,dx$?
Shuffle: yes
- [x] $1$
- [ ] $2$
- [ ] $\tfrac{1}{2}$
? The antiderivative is $x^2$, evaluated from $0$ to $1$.
```

Fill in the blank (accepted answers are separated by `|`):

```quiz
Q: Water is made of hydrogen and ____.
A: oxygen | O
? H2O: two hydrogen, one oxygen.
```

After you press Check, the answer is shown and a Reset button lets you try again (shuffled quizzes reshuffle).
