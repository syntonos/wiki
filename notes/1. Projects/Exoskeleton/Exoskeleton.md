# Exoskeleton
A lower body exoskeleton. Made with Sophie Di and Alex Elia. 

This is an interesting project to consider, because in theory, it's really not that complex, but in practice, there's more than a few interlocking parts, it's an intensely interdisciplinary project (MechE, ECE, CS), and quite a few of those parts are fairly difficult.
